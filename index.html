Phase 1 — 2D editor

Sketch a top-down path. Nodes connect into one continuous C¹-smooth spline.

Controls

  • Place — tap empty space to append a node.
  • Edit — drag a node to move it, or a handle tip to reshape the curve.
  • Delete — tap a node to remove it.
  • Slope — a side profile of the descent, shaped as its own bezier curve.

3D mode

2D editing is locked here; bank, steepness, resolution, and sampling stay adjustable. Press Play to drop a marble.

Camera

One finger to orbit, two fingers to pan, pinch (or scroll) to zoom.

Desktop: WASD flies the camera (forward/back/strafe) while this view is open.

Recording

Captures 15 seconds of Box3D's native world-state recording and downloads it. Starts the simulation if it isn't already running.

Bank (twist handles)

With Edit on, tap a red node to reveal its dial, then drag to roll the rails around their own direction of travel — banking a curve so a fast marble gets pushed inward instead of skipping off.

Sampling

Uniform-t (the default) packs more samples into tight curves; arc-length spaces samples evenly by real distance instead.

Collider note

Each rail collides as a single static triangle mesh, matching the visual tube exactly. See BUILD.txt next to the vendored box3d-wasm build for how.

Contact tuning

Lower hertz or higher damping ratio gives a contact more "give" before it pushes back — these two retune the whole physics world, so any marbles in flight reset. Friction and restitution apply per-rail and don't reset anything.

QC checklist

  • No jittering, snagging, or falling through the rails.

  • Banking a tight curve keeps a fast marble on track through it.
