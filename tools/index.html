Engine note: in this box3d-wasm build, a spinning (kinematic) capsule reliably delivers a glancing/transient push, but a marble cannot stably rest on top of one -- it eventually slips through. So "spin the rail" is a genuine experiment (it may just drop marbles), while "spin the posts" is the mechanism most likely to actually carry marbles up the static rail's slope, the same way the paddles do in the main app. Tune away.
